# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, welcome different levels of
experience, and avoid harassment, discrimination, publication of personal data,
or destructive interference with the archive.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports should use the private contact
described in `SECURITY.md`; they will be handled confidentially and without
retaliation. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and reviewed
by at least two maintainers once the project has more than one maintainer.
